1. Who we are

ClipLens ("we", "us", "our") is an AI-powered media intelligence platform operated by Soham Maskara. References to "you" or "your" mean the organisation using ClipLens and the individual users associated with that organisation.

2. What we collect

Account information

  • Organisation name, subdomain slug, and administrator email address
  • User accounts inside your tenant: username, email, role (admin / editor / viewer)
  • Password hashes (never the password itself)

Billing information

  • Plan subscription and top-up purchase history
  • Stripe Customer ID, Subscription IDs, and invoice references — payment card data is held only by Stripe, never on our servers
  • Total bytes stored and AI compute minutes consumed (for quota enforcement and billing)

Media you upload

  • Video files, photo files, audio tracks, subtitles, and thumbnails
  • Metadata extracted from the media: EXIF (GPS, device, capture date), duration, resolution
  • AI-derived metadata generated by ClipLens (transcripts, face embeddings, scene descriptions, object labels)

Usage data

  • Activity log inside your tenant (who uploaded what, who deleted what) for audit purposes
  • Anonymous server access logs (IP, user agent, request path) retained briefly for security
  • Contact-form submissions on the public marketing page

3. How we use it

We process the information above to:

  • Provide the ClipLens service — run AI analysis, generate transcripts, host video playback
  • Authenticate users and enforce role-based access controls
  • Bill your organisation accurately and process payments through Stripe
  • Enforce plan limits and notify you when you approach a quota
  • Diagnose service errors and prevent abuse
  • Respond to your support requests

We do not use your media or metadata to train AI models, to advertise to you, or to compile profiles about your end users.

4. AI processing of your media

ClipLens uses several open-source AI models — Whisper, CLIP, InsightFace, BLIP, NLLB-200, YOLO, PANNs, and pyannote — to analyse your media. These models run locally on the same infrastructure that hosts your account (or on your own servers if self-hosted).

Your media files and their derived metadata are never sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, or any other external AI provider. The model weights themselves are downloaded once to our servers; your data does not leave the boundary of your tenant.

Face recognition deserves special mention: ClipLens computes mathematical embeddings of faces found in your media so it can group recurring people. These embeddings are stored only inside your tenant database and are never used outside your organisation's library.

5. When we share information

We share information only with the limited set of service providers required to run ClipLens:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe receives your billing address and card details directly; we receive only a customer ID and metadata about the transaction.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers — for hosted deployments only. We host on reputable providers (AWS / GCP / DigitalOcean depending on region) under their standard data-processing terms.
  • Legal authorities — only when compelled by valid legal process, and only to the minimum extent required. We will attempt to notify you unless legally prohibited.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not engage in behavioural advertising.

6. Where data is stored

By default, ClipLens stores your data in a database and media volume located in the region you choose during onboarding. Each organisation gets its own isolated PostgreSQL database and its own media folder — cross-tenant queries are physically impossible.

Self-hosted deployments store all data on your own infrastructure under your full control. We never have access to a self-hosted instance unless you explicitly grant it for support.

7. How long we keep things

  • Active account data — kept for as long as your subscription is active.
  • Media files — kept until you delete them or your account is closed. Soft-deleted items are recoverable for 30 days, then permanently removed.
  • Billing records — retained for 7 years for tax and accounting compliance.
  • Server access logs — retained for 30 days, then automatically purged.
  • Closed accounts — all tenant data (DB + media) is deleted within 30 days of account closure, except billing records as noted above.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, UK DPA, etc.), you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request erasure of your account and data
  • Portability — export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object — to certain types of processing

Email us at privacy@cliplens.com to exercise any of these rights. We respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies & analytics

The ClipLens app uses one essential cookie — a session cookie issued by Django after you log in. It cannot be disabled because authentication won't work without it.

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising cookies on the application. The public marketing site may use first-party analytics in the future (anonymous, aggregated) to understand which features visitors care about; we'll update this policy if that changes.

10. Security

We protect your data with industry-standard practices:

  • TLS in transit for all client connections
  • Disk-level encryption at rest on hosted deployments
  • Per-tenant database isolation — no cross-tenant queries possible
  • Stripe handles all card data — we never see or store it
  • Password hashing with Django's PBKDF2 (SHA256, 600k iterations)
  • Role-based access controls inside each tenant
  • Audit logging of administrative actions

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe you've discovered a security issue, please email security@cliplens.com before public disclosure. We do not currently run a paid bug bounty but we will publicly acknowledge responsibly-disclosed issues.

11. Children's data

ClipLens is a B2B platform intended for organisational use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided data to us through a contact form, email privacy@cliplens.com and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be notified to organisation admins by email at least 30 days before they take effect.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or how we handle your data? Email privacy@cliplens.com.
