The problem isn't storage. It's findability.

Years of interviews, events, training sessions, and product shoots sit on drives and servers — named by date or job number, indexed only by whoever was there that day.

When your team needs a specific moment, they start guessing. Folder by folder. Drive by drive. Until they give up and reshoot it.

ClipLens makes the archive you already own searchable. Self-hosted: files stay exactly where they are — ClipLens reads directly from your storage. Cloud: upload once, your originals stay yours, export everything at any time.
