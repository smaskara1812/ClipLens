What's inside

One upload, every kind of search

Every file you upload runs through a multi-stage AI pipeline. Nothing for your team to configure.

Adaptive streaming

Automatic HLS encoding in every resolution from 144p to 4K, with scrub-thumbnail sprite sheets so users seek smoothly across hours of footage.

Word-level transcripts

Whisper transcribes every word with timestamps. Search a phrase, jump to the exact second it was spoken. Captions ship to the player automatically.

Translation to 200+ languages

One click translates subtitles using Meta's NLLB-200. No re-encoding needed — players show a language picker.

Face recognition

InsightFace clusters faces across your whole library. Tag a person once — find every clip they appear in, forever.

Semantic visual search

Type "sunset over the city" — CLIP returns matching frames even when no caption mentions sunsets. Search what you see, not what you remember.

Geo + named places

Photos with GPS land on an interactive map. Tag your sites once and find media by location.

Speaker diarization

Pyannote tags who is speaking when. Search by person AND topic together — "Sarah talking about Q3 revenue".

Audio event detection

PANNs detects applause, laughter, music, silence — useful highlights surface automatically across long-form footage.

Tenant isolation

Per-organisation database and media folder. Your videos never share infrastructure with another client's. Self-host or use ours.

Visual + audio redaction

Blur faces, mute voices, and scrub transcripts of names or PII — applied as policies that auto-protect new uploads. Render a fully baked-in MP4 when you need to share externally.

Flexible storage

Default to local disk, point a tenant at a NAS mount, an SSHFS volume, or a cloud bucket via FUSE — switch between them with a live, verified migration. No re-uploads, no link rot.

GDPR & audit log

Right-to-be-forgotten in one click — purges face data, embeddings, and crops while logging the action for compliance. Every sensitive operation appears in a tamper-evident audit trail.

Privacy + compliance

Storage

Pick where your media lives — change your mind later

Every tenant gets its own storage root. Start on local disk, move to a NAS or a cloud bucket whenever you outgrow it. ClipLens migrates the files with verification, a 24-hour rollback window, and an audit log.

Local SSD

Default, fast, simple

Stored on the application server's local disk. Sub-millisecond random reads — best for hot playback and active editing workflows.

When: dev, small teams, anything under a few TB.
Mounted NAS / SAN

Shared network volume

Point a tenant at any absolute path — NFS, SMB, iSCSI, or local RAID. Pre-flight checks verify accessibility, writability, and free space.

When: on-prem deployments, regulated industries, existing storage infrastructure.
Remote SSHFS

Any SSH-reachable server

Mount a DAM server or remote host as a local path. Credentials live at the OS level — never in the app, never in the database.

When: off-site archive, hybrid setups, legacy DAM integration.
Cloud bucket via FUSE

S3, GCS, Azure, R2

Mount any object store with rclone/s3fs and treat it like a local folder. Object lifecycle policies handle long-term cold storage.

When: elastic scaling, multi-region redundancy, large libraries.

Switching is non-destructive — the old data stays in place for 24 hours after migration so you can roll back if anything looks off. Talk to us about your setup →

Common questions

Things buyers usually ask

If your question isn't here, the contact form below lands directly in my inbox.

How does the AI processing actually work — do you send our media to OpenAI?
No. Every AI model runs locally inside your tenant — Whisper for transcription, InsightFace for faces, CLIP for visual search, NLLB-200 for translation, YOLO for objects. Nothing is sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, or any third-party AI service. Your media stays inside your infrastructure (or ours, depending on deployment model).
Can we self-host ClipLens on our own servers?
Yes — self-hosting is a first-class deployment option. We provide the full Django + Celery + PostgreSQL stack with deployment scripts. Recommended specs: 32 GB RAM, 8 CPU cores, and a GPU for faster AI processing (CPU-only works too but slower). Contact us for an enterprise self-host license.
What file formats and sizes do you support?
Videos: MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WebM, M4V (anything FFmpeg can read) — up to 4 GB per file by default. Photos: JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, RAW (with EXIF + GPS extraction). Larger limits available on request.
What languages are supported for transcription and translation?
Whisper transcribes ~100 languages with auto-detection. NLLB-200 translates between any pair of 200+ languages. You can ship a single video with captions in 10 languages and the player will let viewers pick — no re-encoding required.
How accurate is the face recognition?
We use InsightFace, currently among the most accurate open face-recognition models. Cosine similarity thresholds are configurable per tenant (default 0.82 for auto-confirm). The system auto-clusters faces and asks you to confirm tags — accuracy improves as your team confirms more matches.
I'm worried about GDPR / BIPA — can we disable biometric processing?
Yes. Each organisation administrator has a control panel at /admin-panel/ai-features/ where they can disable face recognition, speaker diarization, or any individual AI feature with a single toggle. Disabling stops new processing immediately — existing data is preserved unless you explicitly erase it. You can also disable features that aren't biometric (translation, scene description, audio events, etc.) the same way, if you want to limit your AI minute consumption.
What if someone asks us to delete their biometric data?
Every person identified in your library has their own detail page with a Forget person button. Clicking it performs an irreversible, audited deletion within 24 hours of: the face embeddings, cropped face images, the identity record, and any linked speaker references. The action is recorded in an audit log with the operator's username and timestamp, so you can prove compliance to a regulator if asked. There's also an Export data button on the same page that produces a ZIP containing everything we hold about that person — useful for GDPR Article 20 (data portability) requests. The forget action can simultaneously enrol an automatic blur + mute redaction policy against that person across every existing and future video.
How does redaction work — do you re-encode every video?
No, redactions are applied live in the player. The original file is untouched. For a face, we overlay a blur box on the HLS stream at the timestamps where that person appears. For a voice, the player mutes (or beeps) the matching audio ranges. For a transcript, the captions and search index are scrubbed so the redacted phrases never surface. If you need to share a video externally with the redactions baked in, one click renders a permanent MP4 — that's the only time we re-encode. Policies can be revoked: removing a policy restores the original instantly because nothing was ever destructively edited.
Where is our media stored — can we use our own storage?
By default each tenant gets an isolated folder on the ClipLens server's local disk. You can point a tenant at any absolute path the operating system can reach: a network share (NFS, SMB), an SSHFS mount to a remote DAM, or a cloud bucket mounted via rclone or s3fs. Switching mid-life is a verified online migration — we copy every file, verify size and counts, and keep the old location for 24 hours so you can roll back. Cloud credentials never enter ClipLens; the mount is set up at the OS level so the app stays storage-agnostic. See the storage options above or ask us about your specific setup.
What exactly is an "AI minute" — how is it measured?
One AI minute = one minute of wall-clock time that a background worker spends running an AI task on your media (HLS encoding, transcription, face recognition, translation, frame analysis, etc.). Pure database operations and live-streaming don't count. We log elapsed time even when a task fails — the compute was real. Full breakdown in our Terms.
How many AI minutes does a typical video consume?
A rough rule of thumb on our hosted infrastructure:
  • ~4 AI minutes per minute of 4K (2160p) video for the full pipeline (HLS + Whisper + face recognition + frame analysis)
  • ~2-3 AI minutes per minute for 1080p video
  • ~2-3 AI minutes per minute for 720p and below
  • +1-2 AI minutes per language for subtitle translation

Example: a 6-minute 4K video with English captions consumes roughly 25 AI minutes. All hosted plans run on the same hardware, so the ratio is consistent regardless of tier.

What happens to AI minutes if a task fails halfway?
We log the elapsed compute time regardless of outcome, since the work was real. But automatic retries are bounded (Celery retries on transient errors only, with a strict retry cap). Most user-facing failures (bad file format, missing audio track, etc.) bail early before consuming significant minutes. Internal infrastructure failures on our end are never billed to you.
Can we customise the platform with our own branding?
White-label branding (custom logo, colours, favicon, email-sender name) is on the roadmap and available on Enterprise plans. Your users see "Your Brand" everywhere, not ClipLens.
What about billing — do unused storage and AI minutes roll over?
AI minute top-up credits roll forward and expire 12 months after purchase. Plan AI minutes reset every month (use-it-or-lose-it). Storage add-ons stay active until you cancel — and cancellation only takes effect at the end of the current billing period, so you never lose paid-for capacity early.
Can multiple people from our team use one account?
Yes — your organisation gets its own subdomain (yourorg.cliplens.com) with three roles: Admin (full control), Editor (upload & manage media), Viewer (read-only). The number of seats depends on your plan.
What's your refund and cancellation policy?
Plan subscriptions can be cancelled anytime from the billing page — you keep access until the end of the paid period. Top-up credits are non-refundable but never expire mid-period. Annual plans (available on request) can be refunded pro-rata within 30 days. No hidden fees.
